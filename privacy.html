Privacy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This is Yuliang Qiao's technical blog. Reading it does not require an account or consent to analytics.

Analytics stays off until you choose to accept it.

Optional analytics on two articles

I am testing Google Analytics on Innovation Tree and Faster Decisions in Complex Systems with LLMs and World Models. The homepage, this privacy page, and the other articles do not load this analytics integration.

The Google tag loads only after you choose Accept analytics. Choosing Reject analytics, or leaving the choice unanswered, keeps it off. Google Analytics helps me understand article visits, referrals, engagement time, and interactions such as scrolling and following links.

Information sent to Google

If you accept, Google may receive the page URL and title, referring page, interaction events, browser and device details, approximate location, and cookie identifiers. The integration keeps advertising storage, advertising user data, and ad personalization consent disabled. It does not ask for your name or email.

Google uses IP addresses during collection, including to estimate location, but says GA4 does not log or store individual IP addresses. Read how Google uses data from sites using its services and Google's privacy policy for its processing practices.

Cookies and your choice

The blog saves your accept or reject preference in browser storage for up to 180 days. If persistent storage is unavailable, it tries session storage; if both are unavailable, you may be asked again on the next page.

After acceptance, Google Analytics uses first-party cookies with names beginning blog_ga to distinguish browsers and sessions. This integration requests a cookie lifetime of up to 180 days; your browser may shorten it.

You can change your choice using the button above or Analytics choices at the bottom of either pilot article. Withdrawing consent removes this integration's accessible analytics cookies and stops future collection. An article with analytics already running reloads to stop its listeners. Events already collected while consent was granted may finish sending during that transition. Withdrawing does not delete information already sent to Google.

Hosting and questions

GitHub Pages hosts this website. Its handling of hosting requests is separate from optional analytics; see the GitHub privacy statement.
